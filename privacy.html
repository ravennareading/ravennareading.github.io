Privacy policy

Ravenna Reading collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective November 12, 2026

Ravenna Reading is a learn-to-read game for young children and the grown-ups who read with them. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Progress and settings

Which lessons are finished, how well each sound and word is known, the books on the shelf, the days you have read together and the sound settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it.

Voices and listening

Inky's lines, the letter sounds, words and book pages are recordings that ship inside the app. Anything without a recording is read by your device's built-in speech voice, which Ravenna Reading uses without recording or sending anything. The app never listens: it does not use the microphone. The grown-up decides when a child has said a sound or read a word.

Purchases

There is nothing to buy inside the app, and no advertising.

Permissions

None. Ravenna Reading doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or internet.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Ravenna Reading from your device removes all of its data. A grown-up can also start over from the grown-ups' page.

This website

This site is plain pages served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no trackers, fonts or scripts from anyone else. GitHub, as the host, may log visits as described in its own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
